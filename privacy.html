Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information JAXDIGITALS ("we", "us") collects when we make and send digital invitations, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to our clients (the people who book us for an event, "hosts") and to the guests they invite.

Who we are

JAXDIGITALS is a digital invitation business in Tanzania, registered as [registered business name]. You can reach us at [email address] or [phone number], [physical address].

What we collect

From hosts, to plan and deliver the event:

  • Your name, phone number and email, and the details of your event (date, time, venue, programme).
  • Photos and text you give us to put on the invitation card.
  • Your guest list: each guest's name, title, phone number and card type (single or double).
  • Records of payments for our service, such as the amount, date and mobile money reference.

About guests, while the invitation is running:

  • Whether your invitation was delivered and seen, as reported by WhatsApp or the SMS network.
  • Your reply (for example "I will attend" or "Sorry, I can't come").
  • The time your card was scanned at the entrance on the day.

We do not collect payment card numbers, and we do not use tracking cookies or advertising tools on this website.

How we use it

  • To design the invitation card and make a personal copy for each guest.
  • To send each guest their invitation, and a reminder before the event, on WhatsApp or by SMS.
  • To record replies, and to show the host and their committee who is coming and who has arrived.
  • To check guests in at the entrance with the QR code on their card.
  • To issue invoices and receipts to hosts, and to answer questions.

We only message guests whose numbers the host gives us, and only about that host's event. We never use guest details for our own marketing.

Who we share it with

We never sell personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • The event host and their committee, who see their own guests' replies and arrivals.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), which delivers WhatsApp messages.
  • NextSMS, which delivers SMS messages in Tanzania.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the servers where the information is stored.
  • Public authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep a guest list, the cards, replies and check-in records for [90] days after the event, so the host can look back at them, and then delete them. Invoices and payment records are kept for as long as Tanzanian tax law requires. A host can ask us to delete their event's data sooner.

How we protect it

Information is stored on secured servers and sent over encrypted connections. Only our staff who work on your event can see it, and committee and usher links work for one event only.

Your choices

  • Guests can decline an invitation with one tap, and will then not receive a reminder.
  • You can ask us to stop messaging you, by replying to our message or contacting us.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

How to delete your data

To ask us to delete your information:

  • Send a WhatsApp message or email to [WhatsApp number] / [email address].
  • Say "Delete my data" and give the phone number the invitation was sent to (and the event name, if you know it).
  • We confirm when it is done, within 30 days.

Children

Our service is for adults who organise events. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: [email address], [phone number], [physical address], Tanzania.
