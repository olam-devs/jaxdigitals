What we do

Invitations, replies and the gate, all in one place

From the first design to the last guest through the door, JAXDIGITALS takes care of your guest list so your family can enjoy the day.

Card design

An elegant card for your wedding, send-off, kitchen party, birthday or company event, in Swahili or English, with your photo and colours.

A personal card for every guest

Each card carries the guest's name and title, the card type (single or double), a card number and its own QR code.

Sent on WhatsApp and SMS

Guests receive their card on WhatsApp with two reply buttons. Guests without WhatsApp get an SMS, so nobody is left out.

Replies and reminders

See who is coming as replies arrive, and send a friendly reminder before the day to guests who said yes or haven't answered.

A live page for the committee

The family and committee follow replies and arrivals on their own phones, without passing spreadsheets around.

QR check-in at the gate

Ushers scan each card with a phone. A card lets in the right number of people, only once, so the gate stays calm and fair.

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Tell us about your event

    Share the date, venue and your guest list. An Excel sheet or a list of names and phone numbers is fine.

  2. Approve your card

    We design the card with your names, photo and colours, and adjust it until you are happy.

  3. We send the invitations

    Every guest gets their own card on WhatsApp or SMS. You watch the replies come in.

  4. Welcome guests on the day

    Ushers scan the QR codes at the gate, and the committee sees arrivals live.
